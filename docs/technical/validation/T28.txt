- A teacher can feature a shared document in either of two slots. A class
  without a share rejects the placement and does not see it.
- A Markdown edit with an existing private image exercises a lost upload
  acknowledgment and a DB failure during the pointer update. The student's
  old reader and featured entry survive both failures. A retry uses the
  same pinned Drive ID and advances the reader and featured title while
  retaining shares and favorites. A replaced TXT downloads the new exact
  bytes. A subsequent metadata-only edit replays with the same result.
- A late upload races a delete. The delete hides the old reader, download,
  favorite, featured entry and image lease. Its tracked files include the
  in-flight upload, which cannot bring the item back. A lost trash response
  and injected DB failure after trash leave an actionable pending status.
  Retrying from the teacher queue verifies all tracked files in the trash
  without trashing them again. The separate TXT stays readable.
