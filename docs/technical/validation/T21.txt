1 passed (5.3s, 3.8s test body).

- The first open starts the clock. The button exposes its expanded state
  and keeps keyboard focus. The panel fits a 390×844 Chromium viewport.
- A Playwright clock checks Mầm, Chồi, Nụ and Hoa at a one-minute goal.
  Pausing freezes elapsed time. The clock resumes, keeps counting while
  hidden, and remains in Hoa after passing the goal. Goals are whole
  minutes in 1–240 and default to 30. Reset brings elapsed time to zero and
  stays stopped when hidden and reopened.
- Switching assignments gets an independent clock. Returning restores
  elapsed time, goal and visibility from sessionStorage for the same
  student and assignment. Running elapsed time includes time away from the
  writing page in the same browser session.
- Clock interaction generates no draft save or submission requests. An
  explicit submit pauses the clock. Its request, Drive Markdown envelope
  and PostgreSQL submission record have no focus duration. There are no
  server-side timer fields for grading, CSV or reporting to consume. The
  CSV and reporting UI suites are later tasks.

The browser time was simulated. Authentication and PostgreSQL were local
services. Submission read-back crossed the application adapter to the local
fake Drive, and live Google Drive was outside the test scope.
