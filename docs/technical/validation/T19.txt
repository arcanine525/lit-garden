1 passed (15.7s) on the full local application stack.

- Acquire grants a 60-second lease, renew extends the same epoch, and a
  different client receives 423. The test expires the lease in PostgreSQL,
  then verifies that renew cannot revive it. Reacquire advances the epoch,
  stale release fails, and current release permits the next writer.
- Two browser contexts authenticate separately as the same student. The
  second sees the synced Markdown and optional explanation read-only and
  confirms a warning before takeover. The fake provider holds the first
  context's upload reply after it creates the candidate. The second context
  takes over, autosaves from the focused last table cell, and commits a new
  head. The first context's delayed reply produces 409 STALE_WRITER. The
  rejected operation and orphan candidate leave the second head unchanged.
- A fresh read crosses the app adapter to the fake Drive and compares the
  full Markdown and explanation with the second context's save request,
  including the final Vietnamese character. The first context downloads
  its separate Unicode Markdown and explanation while offline, again after
  reconnect, and from IndexedDB on reopening. A different assignment has
  no access to that copy. A fake 504 on Drive read shows the last local
  synced snapshot as read-only while retaining the unsynced device copy.
- The teacher sees no student draft link or editor and receives 403 for
  draft GET and takeover. After assignment closure the student can read
  their head and cannot acquire a writer lease.

The test uses the production Next.js build, local Keycloak and PostgreSQL,
HTTP to the local fake Drive server, Chromium with two browser contexts and
synthesized Unicode keyboard insertion. Google Drive live, a second physical
device and a Vietnamese IME were outside this run. T13 and T34 track live
Google verification. IndexedDB keeps a synced snapshot and unsynced branches
per student and assignment. Restoration is explicit after takeover or reopen.
